About Tiny Arcade

Tiny Arcade is a free browser gaming platform. Every game runs entirely in your web browser — there is nothing to download, nothing to install, and no account to create. Open a game, press Start, and play.

The games are original works, written in plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. New games are added regularly.
