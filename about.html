About Tiny Arcade

Tiny Arcade is a small collection of free mini games that run entirely in your web browser. There is nothing to download, nothing to install, and no account to create. Open a game, press Start, and play.

What you can play

The arcade opens with four games: Fruit Slice, a fast swipe-and-slice game; Snake, the timeless classic; Brick Breaker, a paddle-and-ball arcade game; and Memory Match, a quick card-matching brain-teaser. Every game works on phones and computers, and your best scores are saved only on your own device.

Who makes it

Tiny Arcade is built and run by Manas Jena, an independent maker based in India. The games are original works written in plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No game on this site copies another game's art, sound, or code.

What is coming

New games are added regularly. If a game does not work on your device, or you have an idea for a game you would like to see here, please use the contact page.

How the site is funded

Tiny Arcade is free to play. In the future, the site may show advertising. Any advertising will be clearly shown as advertising, and it will never ask you to install anything or enter personal details to keep playing.
