Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026.

Tiny Arcade ("the site") is a free mini-games website. This policy explains what data the site does — and does not — collect.

No accounts, no personal data

The site does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, or any other personal information to play. We do not collect, store, or sell personal data about visitors.

Best scores stay on your device

When you play a game, your best score may be saved in your own browser using local storage. The same applies to your favourite games, recently played games, and theme choice. That information never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it. The site has no server-side record of your scores or preferences.

Cookies and analytics

The site itself does not set advertising cookies and does not run visitor analytics. Your browser may still store ordinary technical data (such as cached files) to make pages load faster.

Advertising (future)

The site does not currently show advertising. If advertising is added in the future, third-party advertising vendors may use cookies to serve ads. When that happens, this policy will be updated to name the vendors and explain how to opt out of personalised advertising, including through Google's Ads Settings.

External links

The site contains share links (for example, WhatsApp, Telegram, and Facebook). Using a share link takes you to that service, whose own privacy policy applies. The site does not control those services.

Children

The games are suitable for general audiences. The site does not knowingly collect personal information from children, because it does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.
