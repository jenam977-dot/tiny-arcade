How to play

Fruit flies up from the bottom of the screen. Slice it before it falls back down.

  • Phone: swipe across the fruit with your finger.
  • Computer: hold the mouse button and drag across the fruit.
  • Each sliced fruit scores 10 points. Slicing several in one swipe adds a combo bonus.
  • Bombs cost a life. A fruit that falls unsliced also costs a life. You have 3 lives.
  • Your best score is saved on this device only.
